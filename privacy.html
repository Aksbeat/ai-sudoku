Privacy Policy

AI Sudoku — Tutor & Puzzles

Last updated: 2026-08-16

Data we collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal information. The app runs entirely on your device.

  • Puzzle progress (your current board, notes, timer) is stored only in your browser's local storage on this device and is never uploaded.
  • Leaderboard: if you submit a daily-puzzle time, we store only the display name you choose, your solve time, and the date. No account, email, or device identifier is collected.

Third parties

There are no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers, or third-party data processors in this app.

Your control

You can clear all locally stored data at any time via your browser/device settings. To have a leaderboard entry removed, contact the developer.

Contact

Questions: open an issue at github.com/Aksbeat/ai-sudoku.
